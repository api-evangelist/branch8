# Branch8

> Branch8 is a digital solutions and managed contracting company headquartered in Hong Kong with operations across Asia (Singapore, Taiwan, Vietnam, Malaysia, Indonesia, Philippines). We provide managed technical squads, e-commerce implementation, CRM/CDP strategy, and AI-augmented operations for mid-market to enterprise companies.

## Services

- [E-Commerce Implementation](https://branch8.com/services/e-commerce): Adobe Commerce, Shopify Plus, marketplace operations (Shopee, Lazada, Amazon)
- [CRM & CDP Strategy](https://branch8.com/services/crm-cdp): Salesforce, HubSpot, Segment, mParticle, Braze, Dynamics 365
- [Digital Product & Mobile](https://branch8.com/services/digital-product): React Native apps, AEM implementation, headless architecture
- [AI & Automation](https://branch8.com/services/ai-automation): AI integration, workflow automation (n8n, Make, Windmill), intelligent operations
- [Data Engineering](https://branch8.com/services/data-analytics): Snowflake, BigQuery, dbt, Fivetran, analytics platforms
- [Managed Squads](https://branch8.com/services/managed-squads): Blended onshore/offshore technical teams on monthly retainer
